Build Bed Limits

Formulated to fit standard A4 sheets with precision registration mounts. Max physical build area: 210 x 297 mm. Height limits fit items up to 45 mm tall.

Curing Engine Emission

Dual LED liquid-cooled curing modules active at 395nm wavelength. Dynamic power control scales intensity step-by-step from 30% to 100% to protect thin plastic overlays from warping.

Registration Accuracy

Micro-stepping carriage rail delivers horizontal positioning accurate down to ±0.05 mm. Eliminates bleed and ensures perfect overlay alignment.

DPI Density Profile

High-density piezo drop-on-demand technology scales resolution up to 1440 x 1440 DPI. Reproduces crisp text vectors down to 3pt font size.

Ink Pigment Properties

Fortivus utilizes flexible high-stretch elastomers that allow substrates to flex up to 180° after curing without fracturing the printed polymer film.

Prepress RIP Processing

Proprietary raster image processors isolate and compile Spot Color vector paths, mapping White backing plates and Varnish layers automatically.
