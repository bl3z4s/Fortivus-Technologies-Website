Core Service

Direct-to-Object UV Printing

Our flagship A4 UV flatbed technology cures ink instantly using UV light, allowing us to print directly onto almost any flat material up to 3mm thick. The result is a vibrant, scratch-resistant, and waterproof finish.

  • Embossed, tactile textures
  • CMYK + White + Varnish capabilities
  • Works on glass, wood, metal, and acrylic
