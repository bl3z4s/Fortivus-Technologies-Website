Premium UV Solutions

From bulk corporate branding to bespoke individual pieces, discover how our technology brings your ideas to the physical world.

Core Service

Direct-to-Object UV Printing

Our flagship A4 UV flatbed technology cures ink instantly using UV light, allowing us to print directly onto almost any flat material up to 3mm thick. The result is a vibrant, scratch-resistant, and waterproof finish.

  • Embossed, tactile textures
  • CMYK + White + Varnish capabilities
  • Works on glass, wood, metal, and acrylic
Specialty Product

Custom Printed Trays

Elevate your brand or home with our premium custom trays. We print edge-to-edge high-resolution graphics on acrylic, wood, and metal rolling or serving trays.

  • Scratch-resistant coating for daily use
  • Perfect for corporate gifts or retail
  • Available in multiple sizes and materials
Specialty Product

Tactile Phone Cases

Turn everyday tech into personalized art. Using our specialized layer-printing technique, we create 3D textured grips and embossed designs on phone cases that look and feel incredible.

  • Raised texture (3D effect) printing
  • Compatible with TPU, silicone, and hard plastics
  • Fade-resistant against daily pocket wear
