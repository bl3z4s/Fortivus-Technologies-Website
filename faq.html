Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about our UV printing services, file preparation, materials, and order process. If you don't see your question, contact us and we'll get back to you.

What file formats do you accept?

We prefer vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) for the highest print quality. High-resolution PNG, TIFF, or JPEG files at 300dpi+ are acceptable for raster artwork. When possible, include embedded fonts or outline text before export.

What materials can you print on?

Our UV printers can print on acrylic, glass, metal, wood, and many plastics. Not all substrates are supported for every finish—please get in touch if you have a specific material or part geometry.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard production typically ships within 5–7 business days after artwork approval. Rush options may be available—contact sales for availability and pricing.

How should I prepare files for best results?

Provide vector artwork when possible. Convert fonts to outlines, include bleed where applicable, and supply images at 300dpi or higher at final print size. Use CMYK color profiles for color-critical jobs. See our technical specifications for templates and export settings.

Do you offer proofs before printing?

Yes — digital proofs are provided for approval on most orders. Physical proofs are available on request for critical color work and may incur additional cost.

What are your shipping and returns policies?

Shipping options are provided at checkout or quoted per order. We inspect all items before shipment—if an item arrives damaged or does not match the approved proof, contact us within 7 days and we'll evaluate replacements or refunds per our terms.
