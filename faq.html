Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you accept?

We prefer vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) for the highest print quality, but high-resolution PNG or TIFF files (300dpi+) are also acceptable.

What materials can you print on?

Our UV printers are versatile and can print on acrylic, glass, metal, wood, and various plastics. Contact us if you have a specific substrate in mind.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard orders typically ship within 5-7 business days. Rush options may be available upon request.
