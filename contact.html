Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you accept for UV prints?

We accept high-resolution raster files (PNG, JPG, TIFF) as well as vector formats (PDF, AI, EPS). For optimal print resolution and zero bleeding, we recommend vector documents or high-DPI images exported at 300 DPI or higher.

How long does flatbed UV curing take?

Standard turnaround for flatbed production is 3-5 business days from layout signoff. Rush processing options are available for emergency projects requiring same-day or next-day turnaround.

Can I provide my own items (BYOC) to print?

Yes! Our Bring Your Own Case (BYOC) service is designed specifically for this. We can print directly onto wood trays, phone blocks, glass panels, or metals up to a maximum height clearance of 45mm.
